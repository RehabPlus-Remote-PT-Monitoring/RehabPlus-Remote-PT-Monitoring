## 2. What information we collect

**Account and contact information**

- Your name, email address, phone number and date of birth.
- Your profile picture, if you choose to add one. It is resized and stored with your profile, and you can change or remove it at any time.
- Your login credentials. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; the Clinic cannot see it.

**Health information (sensitive personal information)**

Under the Data Privacy Act, information about your health is *sensitive personal information* and receives a higher level of protection. We process:

- Your condition, medical summary and therapy plan, as recorded by your therapist.
- Assessments, treatment goals and clinical notes written by the Clinic.
- The exercises prescribed to you, including targets such as sets, repetitions and schedule.
- Your exercise results: start and end times, repetitions and sets completed, repetitions the App did not count, form-compliance scores, whether you ended a session early or exercised outside your schedule.
- The pain level (0 to 10) and any notes you enter after each session.

**Messages and notifications**

- Messages you exchange with your therapist and with the Clinic, with the time they were sent.
- Problem reports you send from Settings. These include the App version and recent error messages from the App, to help fix the problem.
- Which notifications you want to receive on your phone (Settings > Notifications), so the Clinic doesn't send ones you turned off.
- Notifications sent to you in the App (for example, new exercises or new messages).

**Device information**

- A push-notification token for each phone you sign in on, so the Clinic can send you notifications. It is deleted when you sign out.

**Your consent record**

- Which version of this Policy and of the Terms and Conditions you accepted, and when.

## 3. How the camera is used

The App uses your phone's front camera during exercise sessions to detect your body position and count your repetitions.

- **Video is analysed on your phone, in real time, using Google ML Kit's on-device pose detection.**
- **Video and images are never recorded, saved, or sent to the Clinic, to Google, or to anyone else.**
- Only the results described in section 2 (such as repetitions counted and form scores) are saved.

The camera is only active while an exercise session is open. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your phone's settings at any time, but the App then cannot track your exercises.

## 4. What we do not collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, files, microphone audio or advertising identifiers. It does not access your photos, except a single picture you choose yourself as your profile picture. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics. Technical diagnostic logs stay on your phone and are only sent if you choose to report a problem. Your other settings (sounds, camera, reminder time, text size) are stored only on your phone.

## 5. Why we use your information

We use your information only to:

- Provide your physical therapy remotely: show your prescribed exercises, guide and count your repetitions, and record your results.
- Let your therapist monitor your progress, adjust your treatment and respond to pain or difficulty you report.
- Let you communicate with your therapist and the Clinic, and send you notifications about your care.
- Manage your account, keep the App secure and prevent misuse.
- Keep clinical records and meet the Clinic's legal and professional obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for marketing or advertising.

The App's repetition counting and form feedback are automated aids. No decision about your treatment is made by the App alone; your therapist reviews your results.

## 6. Our legal basis

We process your information on these grounds under the Data Privacy Act:

- **Your consent**, given when you accept this Policy in the App (Sections 12(a) and 13(a)).
- **Medical treatment**: processing is necessary for your treatment by a health professional and medical treatment institution, with adequate protection in place (Section 13(c)).
- **Our legal obligations**, such as keeping clinical records for the period the law requires.

## 7. Who can see your information

- **Your assigned therapist** can see your health information, exercise results and messages.
- **Clinic administrators** can see all patient records to manage the Clinic's services.
- **Front-desk staff** can see your contact details and messages sent to the Clinic's general inbox, but not your clinical records.
- **Google LLC**, through Google Firebase, stores and transmits the App's data on the Clinic's behalf as a **personal information processor**. It provides Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (the database) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), under Google's data processing terms. Google does not use this data for advertising.

All Clinic personnel with access are bound by professional confidentiality.

We disclose your information to anyone else only if you ask us to, or if the law requires it (for example, a court order or a lawful request from a government authority).

## 8. Where your information is stored

The App's database is hosted by Google Cloud in Singapore (region asia-southeast1). Sign-in and push-notification services run on Google's global infrastructure. This means your information is transferred outside the Philippines. The Clinic remains accountable for it, and Google is bound by contract to protect it to a standard comparable to the Data Privacy Act.

## 9. How we protect your information

We use organisational, physical and technical measures, including:

- Encryption of all data in transit (TLS) and at rest (Google Cloud encryption).
- Secure sign-in through Firebase Authentication; the Clinic never sees or stores your password.
- Database security rules that let you access only your own records, and let staff access only what their role requires.
- Exercise records and messages cannot be altered or deleted from the App once saved.
- Disabling the login of archived accounts.
- Restricting server credentials to authorised Clinic systems.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at real risk of serious harm, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by NPC rules.

You can help by keeping your password secret, locking your phone, and signing out on shared devices.

## 10. How long we keep your information

We keep your health and exercise records for as long as you are a patient and afterwards for {{RETENTION_PERIOD}}, or longer if the law requires it. After that, your information is securely deleted or anonymised.

If your account is archived, your login is disabled but your records are kept for the same period, because they form part of your clinical record.

Push-notification tokens are deleted when you sign out.

## 11. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your information is processed (this Policy).
- **Access** your information and obtain a copy of it.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate or incomplete. You can update your name, phone number, date of birth and profile picture in the App yourself (**Profile > Privacy & Legal > Account Info**).
- **Object** to processing, and **withdraw your consent** at any time.
- **Erasure or blocking** of information that is no longer necessary, unlawfully obtained or processed without a lawful basis.
- **Data portability**: obtain your information in a commonly used electronic format.
- **Damages**, if you suffer harm from inaccurate, incomplete, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of your information.
- **File a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission (www.privacy.gov.ph).

To exercise any of these rights, message the Clinic through **Help & Support** in the App, or email our DPO at {{DPO_EMAIL}}. We may ask you to verify your identity first. We will respond without undue delay.

**Withdrawing consent.** You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and the Clinic may still keep records it is required by law to keep. Because remote monitoring cannot work without processing your health information, withdrawing consent means your use of the App will end; your therapist will discuss other options for your care.

## 12. Minors

The App is intended for patients aged 18 and over. A patient under 18 may use it only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who accepts this Policy on the patient's behalf and may exercise the patient's rights.
